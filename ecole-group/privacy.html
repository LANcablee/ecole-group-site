Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This policy explains how Ecole Group Ltd (“we”, “us”, “our”) handles personal information in connection with this website. We are committed to handling your data openly, lawfully and in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Who we are

Ecole Group Ltd is a distribution company registered in England and Wales under company number {{ECOLE_COMPANY_NUMBER}}. Our registered office is at {{ECOLE_REGISTERED_OFFICE}}. Ecole Group Ltd is the data controller for the personal information described in this policy. For any privacy question, contact us at info@ecolegroup.co.uk.

What we collect

This website has no contact forms, accounts or logins, and it does not collect personal information itself. We only receive personal information if you choose to email us — in which case we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

As with most websites, our hosting and security provider may automatically process limited technical data (such as an IP address) for the sole purpose of keeping the site secure and running. This is not used to identify you for marketing.

Fonts and third-party services

All fonts and other assets on this website are served directly from our own hosting (“self-hosted”). No content is loaded from third-party font or content-delivery services such as Google Fonts, and no personal data — including your IP address — is transmitted to any such service when you visit this site.

Why we use your information

We use the information you send us solely to respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, to take steps at your request. Our lawful bases under UK GDPR are your consent in choosing to contact us and our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries.

Cookies

This website does not set any cookies and does not use cookies for analytics, tracking or advertising. Our hosting and security provider may set a small number of strictly necessary cookies to protect the site; under UK rules these do not require consent. See our Cookie Policy for details.

Sharing your information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may rely on trusted service providers (for example, our email and website hosting providers) who process data on our behalf under appropriate safeguards. Where any provider processes data outside the United Kingdom, we ensure appropriate safeguards recognised by UK data protection law are in place.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry correspondence only for as long as necessary to deal with your request and for our legitimate business records, after which it is deleted.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection to processing. To exercise these, email info@ecolegroup.co.uk. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised date.
