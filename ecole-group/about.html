About

Ecole Group is an independent group based in the United Kingdom.

We establish brands within the group, and we take a small number of others into our representation — often from beyond the UK — giving each a considered route to the British market.

We are deliberately selective. Rather than carry many lines, we take on a small number of brands and commit to each one fully: dependable supply, presentation that respects the product, and a close working relationship with the retailers who stock it.

Sardis Home, our cotton home textile brand, is the first established within the group. ELY’S CURE, a natural skincare range from Türkiye / Turkey, is the first to come under our representation. The chemicals sector will follow, and only when it can be done to the same standard.
